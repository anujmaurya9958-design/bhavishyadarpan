Numerology — Mulank & Bhagyank

Two numbers hidden in your date of birth reveal your inner nature and your life's direction. Each number is ruled by a graha.

मूलांक · Mulank
Your Root Number

Mulank comes from the day of your birth, reduced to a single digit. It shows your basic nature, instincts and how others see you.

Example: born on the 27th → 2 + 7 = 9, so Mulank is 9.

भाग्यांक · Bhagyank
Your Destiny Number

Bhagyank comes from the complete date of birth (day, month and year), added and reduced to a single digit. It points to your life path and the lessons you are here to learn.

Example: 27-10-2000 → 2+7+1+0+2+0+0+0 = 12 → 1 + 2 = 3, so Bhagyank is 3.
